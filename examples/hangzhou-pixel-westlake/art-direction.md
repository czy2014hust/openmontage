# Art Direction — 像素版杭州·西湖夜游

## Design read
A dreamy game-city postcard of Hangzhou at night: readable landmarks, water-first, code-made voxel fly-through.

## Palette
- Sky: deep indigo `#0B1C3A` → dusk violet `#1A2B55`
- Water: jade-teal `#1F6F6A` with soft specular
- Lights: lantern gold `#FFB454`, window warm `#FFCF7A`
- Architecture: muted brick `#6B4E3D`, stone `#8A8F98`, pagoda timber `#8B3A2A`

## Signature device
West Lake mirror + Leifeng Pagoda vertical silhouette; continuous parallax dolly (no hard cuts).

## Anti-patterns
No stock Remotion scene cards, no CapCut mask wipe, no purple neon default AI look.
